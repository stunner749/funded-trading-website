FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

QUESTIONS? WE HAVE ANSWERS.

What is a funded trading challenge?

It is an evaluation that tests whether you can follow the trading rules and reach the required profit target.

How long does the challenge take?

There is no fixed completion time, but you must complete at least five trading days.

What happens if I reach the profit target?

If you meet the target without exceeding the maximum loss, your challenge will be marked as passed.

Can I trade from any country?

Yes, traders from supported countries can participate after meeting the account and verification requirements.
